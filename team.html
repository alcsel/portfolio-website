// History

Club history

Sakaryaspor was founded in 1954, at the same time as Sakarya became a province. The club quickly became a familiar name in Turkish football and has competed in several divisions over the decades.

Playing in green and black, Sakaryaspor has been one of the important representatives of football in the region and has contributed a great deal to the sporting culture of the city through its supporters.
