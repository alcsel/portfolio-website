// Places to visit

Highlights

🏞 Lake Sapanca

One of the largest freshwater lakes in Turkey and part of Istanbul's drinking water supply. Ideal for hiking and camping.

🌿 Kartepe

A district chosen for winter sports and summer tourism alike, known for its ski slopes and hiking trails.

🏰 Adapazari Clock Tower

The historic clock tower that became the symbol of the city, standing in the centre of Adapazari.

🌊 Kizilcik Beach

A natural beach on the Black Sea coast where green forest meets the water.

🕌 New Mosque

A historic mosque in central Adapazari that carries the marks of Ottoman architecture.

🏛 Sakarya University

One of the established universities of Turkey and the education centre of the region, with a large campus and a broad academic staff.

// About the city

History of Sakarya

Sakarya takes its name from the river that runs through the province. The Battle of Sakarya, one of the decisive engagements of the Turkish War of Independence, was fought on this land. The city became a province in 1954 and is today both an industrial and a tourism centre.

Economy & industry

Sakarya hosts the Toyota factory and holds an important position in the automotive sector. Agriculture, textiles and the food industry also play a critical role in the economy of the city.
